# Direction contract (development only, never shipped)

Surfaces: simulated Echo Show (`/echo`, mode Experience with Operate rules) and family page
(`/family`, mode Operate). Chosen by the owner on 2026-10-01 from three options (US road
signs, pharmacy label, standard health app). The skill launcher was not installed, so no seed
key exists; the roll was replaced by the owner's explicit choice.

**THESIS.** The guardian speaks the language every American driver already reads under
stress: road signs. A warning sign is a yellow diamond, the next step is a green guide sign,
help is a blue services sign, red means only 911. It refuses the soft blue health app card.

**OWN WORLD.** Echo: asphalt ground (#1F2326), sign white type, MUTCD style yellow diamonds
with black legends and an inset border, highway green guide panels, services blue, stop red
for 911 only. Overpass (highway lettering lineage) for sign legends, uppercase; Atkinson
Hyperlegible Next for sentences. Family page: daylight white ground, the same signs as small
markers, green primary actions, rectangular controls with 8 px corners.

**STORY.** The older adult sees that what they felt has a name, sees one next step, and
does it. The family organizer sees who is saved, what happened, and what to fix.

**FIRST VIEWPORT.** Echo: caption of Alexa's last sentence large in the upper half; a row of
up to three yellow diamonds under it, raised one by one as Alexa names them; one green guide
panel at the bottom with the next step; the light ring along the bottom edge. Family page:
the older adult's name as the heading, then the people list, then one green "Add a person".

**FORM.** Road signage system (MUTCD vernacular), position 1 of 3 presented options.

**FINISH.** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish
review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Scene: Echo Show on a kitchen counter, read from one or two meters, any hour, by someone who
just hung up a frightening call: dark ground with retroreflective style signs. Family page:
an adult child on a phone in daylight: light by default, dark when the system asks.
